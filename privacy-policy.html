Privacy Policy — What Should I Do?

Last updated: 2026-07-17

What Should I Do? ("WSID", "the app", "we") is a personal reflection app. This policy explains what data we handle and why. We keep it minimal by design.

What we collect

  • Anonymous identifier. On first launch the app creates an anonymous account (a random user ID). No name or email is required to use the app.
  • Account email (optional). If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address associated with that account, so your history can follow you across devices. You can use the app fully without signing in.
  • Your reflections. The questions, options, and the reaction you record during a session are stored so you can keep your own history. This content is private to your account.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or device advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To provide the core experience (create and store your reflection sessions).
  • To manage your account (anonymous or signed-in).

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

Where it's stored

Data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL), encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is restricted so that each user can read and write only their own rows.

Your control — deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account. This is an immediate, irreversible hard delete of your profile and reflection history. Anonymous data is wiped the same way.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is intended for users 18 and over.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact: dev.autobi.io@gmail.com
